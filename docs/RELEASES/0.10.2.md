# Axiom 0.10.2

**Release date:** 2026-11-09

Corrects an overstated claim in the threat-sharing note added in 0.10.1.

## What was wrong

The 0.10.1 note said that "every confirmed indicator makes the shared Blackbox graph stronger" and
that "a stronger graph means Axiom - and every other node - recognises malicious infrastructure
earlier". Two parts of that did not hold:

- A report is a **sighting**, not a confirmed threat. Blackbox computes a stage for it locally:
  an uncorroborated report from an unlisted reporter is `reported · monitor`, a counted author can
  reach `flag`, and corroboration needs distinct reporters over time. A single report does not
  "strengthen" the graph by itself.
- **Axiom does not consume the community graph for detection.** Axiom's firewall, Shield and Privacy
  Shield decide from local rules and lists. Blackbox's agent guard is what can flag a community
  threat, and community threats never block by themselves: blocking belongs to the curated verified
  tier.

## What the note says now

> Reports join the shared Blackbox graph as community sightings: independent nodes can corroborate the
> same indicator, which lets Blackbox flag it across the network and gives the curators evidence to
> promote it into the verified blocking tier. Axiom's own firewall and DNS filtering still decide only
> from local lists and rules.

The styling is unchanged: success-coloured text with a success bar on the palette's alternate surface,
so it follows Dark, Light, Dark Midnight and Light Warm.
