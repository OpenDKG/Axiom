# Axiom 0.59.0

**Released:** 16 November 2026

0.59.0 introduces the darker folded brushed-titanium Axiom brand mark, reorganizes the sidebar navigation and `Network Dashboard` tabs, deepens `Axiom Glass Dark`, and harmonizes controls, graphs, alerts, and dialogs across all six Avalonia desktop themes.

## What changed

- **Dark folded titanium brand mark & centered sidebar chrome:**
  - Updated `axiom-icon.png` and `axiom-lockup.png` in both UI asset folders (`src/Axiom.UI.Avalonia/Assets` and `src/Axiom.UI/Resources`), rebuilt multi-size `axiom.ico`, and regenerated MSIX Store tiles and splash assets (`packaging/msix/generate-assets.ps1`): removed the stray upper-right shield notch, refined the crisp diagonal metal fold (`brushed steel` vs `dark gunmetal`), matched the folded titanium finish across the `AXIOM` letters, and added subtle `xiom` lettering below `A` inside the shield.
  - Updated `MainWindow.axaml` to remove the redundant top-left version label, center the top-left `AXIOM` lockup and `LOCAL-FIRST · NO TELEMETRY` chip, center the bottom-left footer (`Axiom` icon, name, version, description, and action buttons), and point the `Contact` button to `mailto:contact@opendkg.org`.
- **Navigation reorganization & compact `Network Dashboard` monitoring tabs:**
  - Consolidated `Live Connections`, `Applications`, and `Network Events` into compact tabs inside `Network Dashboard` (`OverviewView.axaml`, `OverviewPageViewModel.cs`), while preserving deep-link page navigation in `ShellViewModel.NavigateToPage`.
  - Removed the duplicate `Privacy Shield` card from `Network Dashboard`, renamed the `PROTECTION` sidebar group to `AI` (containing `Agent Protection`, `Agent Activity`, `Agent Health`, and `AI Analyst`), and grouped `Network Control`, `Privacy Shield`, and `Integrations` under `NETWORK & PRIVACY`.
- **Glass Dark depth & full cross-theme control/dialog harmonization:**
  - Removed opaque dark list/scroll backgrounds in `Live Connections` and `Network Events` (`EventsView.axaml`, `OverviewView.axaml`) so glass themes preserve frosted translucency, and deepened the `Axiom Glass Dark` backdrop veil (`AxiomPalette.axaml`, `MainWindow.axaml.cs`).
  - Styled `CheckBox`, `NumericUpDown` spinners, `Separator`, and high-contrast `Button.danger` states in `AxiomControls.axaml`; darkened `Axiom.Warning` to `#B45309` in `Light` and `GlassLight`; wrapped `umanitek-logo.png` cleanly in `AgentsView.axaml`; made `ThreatGraphControl` resolve theme brushes dynamically; and added `AxiomDialogTheme` so dialogs, setup prompts, first-run windows, and toast alerts (`AxiomAlertService`, `AvaloniaUserInteraction`, `AvaloniaBlackboxDialogs`, `AvaloniaSecurityDialogs`, `FirstRunDialog`) track the active theme.
